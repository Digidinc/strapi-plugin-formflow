Add Telegram notifications (Pro). Configure a customer-owned bot connection in FormFlow Settings, then enable a per-form rich-message template that posts to a Telegram chat, group, or channel after each final submission. Delivery is outbound-only and fire-and-forget: FormFlow never installs a webhook, reads updates, or delays the public submission response.
